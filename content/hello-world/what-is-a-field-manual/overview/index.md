A CAI Field Manual is the one thing you want in your pack when you head into the field. It tells you what to do, in which order, and why you might want to do that instead of something else. It is rugged, and there might be some hand-written notes in the margins.

It contains knowledge, tips, tricks, workarounds, warnings, and other time-saving advice accumulated from your peers — people who have already been where you are right now, or in situations similar enough to be useful.

## Who writes it

A CAI field manual is first and foremost what CAI create for themselves as they come to grips with new versions, new concepts, new challenges, and new jungle. Trial and error, figuring out where the dragons are and how to shortcut through technological complexity — markers are laid down along the way so the path can be found again, more efficiently.

It is a collaborative effort: you get back what you put into it.

## Where it came from

The name "CAI Field Manual" was not chosen on day one. It came out of different initiatives, ideas, directions, fits and starts, failed attempts, doubts, and tribulations. It will keep evolving.

## Why it is open

This is not held as a mythical "secret sauce." It is made available to those who need it — because it is the right thing to do, and because this is what Open Source is all about. Everything here stands on work other people shared.
