A CAI Field Manual tells you what to do, in which order, and why you might do that instead of something else. It is rugged, and there might be some hand-written notes in the margins.

Inside is what your peers learned before you: knowledge, tips, tricks, workarounds, warnings. People who have already been where you are, or somewhere close enough that it counts.

## Who writes it

CAI write these manuals for themselves, as they come to grips with new versions and new jungle. Trial and error, wrong turns, shortcuts that worked; markers get laid down along the way, so the path is faster to find the next time.

It is a collaborative effort: you get back what you put into it.

## Where it came from

The name "CAI Field Manual" was not chosen on day one. It came out of earlier initiatives, fits and starts, and failed attempts, and it will keep evolving.

## Why it is open

Nothing here is held back as a "secret sauce." The manual is open because it is the right thing to do, and because everything in it stands on work other people shared.
